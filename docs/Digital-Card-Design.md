# Digital Card Design

## 1. Overview

The digital employee card is designed as a digital version of the TeleGlobal employee visiting card / ID card.

The card contains a front side and back side.

## 2. Front Side

The front side contains:

- TeleGlobal logo
- Company name
- Employee photograph
- Employee full name
- Designation
- Department / Business Unit
- Employee ID
- QR code

## 3. Back Side

The back side contains:

### Employee Information

- Full name
- Designation
- Employee ID
- Official email
- Contact number
- Emergency contact
- Office location
- LinkedIn
- Instagram
- Facebook
- Blood group
- Date of birth

### Company Information

- Company logo
- Company name
- About company
- Website
- Corporate address
- Other locations
- Contact number
- Contact email
- CEO
- Founder
- Mission
- Vision

### Company Assets

- Badges
- Certificates
- Company deck

## 4. Responsive Design

The digital card must work on:

- Mobile phones
- Tablets
- Desktop browsers

## 5. Company Logo

The company logo should link to the official TeleGlobal website.

## 6. QR Code

The QR code provides access to the employee's permanent digital card URL.

## 7. Visual Design

The interface should maintain a professional corporate appearance with:

- Clear typography
- Consistent spacing
- Corporate blue styling
- Proper image proportions
- Responsive layout
- Front/back card presentation
