# User Roles

## 1. ADMIN

The administrator manages the organization and employee information.

### Admin Permissions

Admin can:

- Login
- View dashboard
- Create employees
- View employees
- Edit employees
- Delete employees
- View employee QR codes
- Download QR codes
- Import employees through Excel
- Manage company profile
- Upload company logo
- Manage company locations
- Manage badges and certificates
- Upload company deck

### Admin Restrictions

Admin functionality is available only to authenticated administrators.

---

## 2. EMPLOYEE

Employees manage their own personal information.

### Employee Permissions

Employees can:

- Login
- View their profile
- Edit their personal details
- Upload/update their profile picture
- View their digital card
- View their QR code

### Employee Restrictions

Employees cannot modify company-level information.

Employees cannot modify:

- Company name
- Company logo
- Company website
- Corporate address
- CEO
- Founder
- Mission
- Vision
- Company locations
- Company badges
- Company deck

---
