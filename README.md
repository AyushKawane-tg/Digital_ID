# TeleGlobal Digital Card Platform

A web-based platform for managing TeleGlobal digital employee visiting cards and employee ID cards.

## Main Capabilities

- Admin and employee authentication
- Employee profile management
- Company profile management
- Employee management
- Company locations
- Badges and certificates
- Company deck
- Excel bulk employee import
- Automatic QR generation
- Permanent digital card URLs
- Responsive digital employee cards
